Feature 4

Emergency Response
Workflow

The critical test: what happens when vitals go critical?

⚠ 16:32 — SpO₂ drops to 88%
16:32 🔴 Alert fires on Command Center. Care Manager notified.
16:33 📱 Family notified: "Nurse dispatched, doctor reviewing."
16:33 👨‍⚕️ Nearest nurse auto-assigned. Navigation + vitals history sent.
16:35 Doctor on-call reviews case remotely. Approves intervention plan.
16:58 ✓ Nurse arrives. Vitals stabilizing. Doctor signs off.
Investor Hook
Alert → nurse dispatched: <3 minutes — vs. hours in traditional systems. This is the difference between a home visit and an ambulance.
